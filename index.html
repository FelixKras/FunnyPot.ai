Deception that produces intelligence

Keep attackers talking. Turn every move into evidence.

FunnyPot is an AI-powered SSH honeypot that presents a convincing synthetic Linux host, captures what happens after authentication, and transforms raw commands into explainable attacker journeys. It helps security researchers see the intent behind the traffic—not just another failed login.
